How to play

    Use several fingers at once. Apple Pencil pressure sets the volume. Looper: tap record, play a groove, tap record again to close the loop, then tap it once more to layer on top. Turn on the metronome first to lock loops to whole bars.

    No sound? Turn off Silent Mode in Control Center (the bell icon), turn the volume up, and check that headphones or a Bluetooth speaker aren't connected. If the page is open inside another app, open it in Safari instead.

    To keep it on the iPad, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
